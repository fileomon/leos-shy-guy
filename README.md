# Leo's SCP-096 Demonstration
Release date: August 8, 2026

Play now: https://www.roblox.com/games/99720263294059/Leos-SCP-096-Demonstration

## About
SCP-096 is a tall pale humanoid entity that is normally docile and calm, but this game puts you to the test of how long you can survive its madness. Basically, just don't look at its face, no matter from which point, or it's game over for you! It can become insanely enraged, and within a few minutes or even seconds, it will pursue and kill its target. Even if it's already enraged, you must not look at its face.. or else you're next!
